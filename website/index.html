WHY THIS EXISTS

Too much potential
to leave behind.

Feeltune abandoned the Rhizome product line, leaving customers worldwide in the dark and without ongoing manufacturer support.

By September 2013, owners were already reporting that the company no longer existed and that essential host software had to be shared between users. Expensive, physically functional hardware was left dependent on software that could no longer be relied on.

“I paid €1500,- for my Rhizome LE as a very early adopter.”

Latif Karoumi
